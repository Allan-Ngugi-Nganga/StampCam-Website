Overview

StampCam at a glance

For press, partners and anyone writing about the app.

StampCam is a professional GPS timestamp camera for iOS. It stamps date, time, GPS coordinates, altitude, heading and custom notes permanently onto every photo, then turns those photos into professional PDF inspection reports.

Tagline: Pay once. Own forever. No subscriptions.
Price: $19.99, one-time purchase.
Platform: iPhone, iOS 17 or later.

What it is

A field documentation tool for people who need photos to hold up as records. Contractors, property managers, insurance adjusters, utility and telecom crews, and delivery and logistics teams use StampCam to capture proof of when and where work happened, without a monthly fee or a cloud account.

The pitch in three lines

  • Burn verified date, time, GPS, altitude, heading and notes onto every photo.
  • Export branded, client-ready PDF reports before you leave the site.
  • No subscriptions, no servers and no tracking, at $19.99 once.

Key facts

  • Developer: StampCam
  • Category: Business, photo and video documentation
  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 17 or later
  • Price model: One-time purchase, $19.99
  • Privacy: Fully on-device, no backend, no analytics

Feature highlights

  • Timestamp engine with verified date and time, GPS, altitude and compass heading.
  • Professional PDF inspection reports with a branded cover sheet and two photos per page.
  • Four camera modes: Standard, Site, Document and AR Compass.
  • NTP time verification and fully customisable watermark overlays.
  • iOS 17 Live Activities, Lock Screen widgets, voice-to-text notes and hashtag grouping.
