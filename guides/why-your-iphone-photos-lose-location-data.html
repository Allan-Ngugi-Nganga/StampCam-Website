Why your iPhone photos lose location data

Updated 5 October 2026 · 4 min read

You take a photo with location on, send it to someone, and the location is gone. This is normal, and it is one of the main reasons a visible stamp matters.

The usual causes

  • Messaging apps. Many strip metadata to save space and protect privacy, so the location never arrives.
  • Export and web tools. Image optimisers and CMS uploads often remove it.
  • Cropping and editing. Some tools drop metadata when they re-save.
  • Screenshots. A screenshot is a new image with no location at all.

How to tell if it is gone

Open the photo, swipe up and look for the map. If there is no map, the location was never recorded or was removed along the way.

The reliable fix

Do not rely on metadata surviving a trip through an app. Burn the location into the image at capture, so it is part of the picture and travels with it everywhere.

Questions

  • Does AirDrop keep location? AirDrop usually keeps full metadata between Apple devices, but that is the exception, not the rule for messaging apps.
  • Is there a setting to stop apps stripping location? No. You control the metadata at the source, not at every app it passes through.
