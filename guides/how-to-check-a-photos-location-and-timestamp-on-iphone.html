How to check a photo's location and timestamp on iPhone

Updated 5 October 2026 · 3 min read

The Photos app shows a photo's captured date, time and location. Knowing where to look is useful, as is knowing what that information cannot tell you.

In the Photos app

  1. Open the photo full screen.
  2. Swipe up, or tap the info button.
  3. You will see the date and time, and a map if location was recorded.
  4. Tap Adjust next to the date to change it, though this does not change the file metadata proof.

What it cannot tell you

  • Whether the photo was edited after capture.
  • Whether the clock was correct at the time.
  • Anything at all once metadata has been stripped.

When you need a stronger record

For work, insurance or legal use, a visible stamp with a network time check gives a record that does not depend on hidden metadata that may already be gone.

Questions

  • Can the date on a photo be faked? Yes. Metadata can be edited and phone clocks can be changed. A network-verified stamp is harder to fake.
