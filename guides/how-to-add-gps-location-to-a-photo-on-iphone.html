How to add GPS location to a photo on iPhone

Updated 5 October 2026 · 4 min read

iPhone photos can carry GPS coordinates, but only if location is enabled for the camera, and the coordinates stay hidden in metadata unless an app draws them on the picture.

Turn on location for the camera

  1. Open Settings, then Privacy and Security, then Location Services.
  2. Tap Camera and choose While Using the App.
  3. Check that Precise Location is on.

Then check that it worked

Open the photo in the Photos app and swipe up. If location was recorded, a map thumbnail appears. If it is missing, the permission is off, or the photo was taken somewhere with no GPS fix.

Making the location visible

Coordinates in metadata are easily lost when a photo is shared. To make the location part of the record, use an app that burns the coordinates, altitude and heading into the image at capture.

Accuracy in the field

  • Give the phone a moment to get a satellite fix before shooting.
  • Precise Location matters more than the rough setting.
  • Indoors, coordinates are less accurate, so a heading and a note help.

Questions

  • Why does my photo have no location? Either Camera location access is off, or the photo was taken without a GPS fix.
  • Can I see coordinates as numbers, not a map? In the Photos app you can, and apps that stamp the image can print the raw latitude and longitude.
