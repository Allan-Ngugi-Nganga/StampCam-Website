Progress photo documentation for renovations

Updated 5 October 2026 · 4 min read

Renovations move fast and change daily. Fixed camera positions turn a pile of photos into a clear record of what changed and when.

Set fixed vantage points

Pick one spot per room and shoot from it every visit. Consistency makes progress obvious and reveals anything that goes wrong.

Before, during and after

  • Before. Existing conditions and anything you are not touching.
  • During. The same views each visit, plus close ups of works in progress.
  • After. Final condition per room, for handover.

Protecting the client relationship

Neighbours and clients remember what was agreed. A dated photo set shows what was there before, which settles most disputes about pre-existing damage.

Questions

  • Do I need to photograph areas I am not working on? Yes. Existing conditions elsewhere prevent blame for damage you did not cause.
  • Should photos go into the client report? Only what is useful. Keep the full set in your own file.
