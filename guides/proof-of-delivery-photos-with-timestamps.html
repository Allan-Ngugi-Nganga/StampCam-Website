Proof of delivery photos with timestamps

Updated 5 October 2026 · 4 min read

Proof of delivery lives or dies on three facts: the drop location, the time, and the condition of the item. All three can be captured in seconds.

The shot sequence

  1. The item at the door or drop point, with the location visible.
  2. Any damage or discrepancy, close up.
  3. A wider shot that shows the address or landmark.
  4. Any paperwork, signed and dated.

What makes it stick

  • A visible date, time and location on every photo.
  • No edits after capture.
  • A short note with the order or reference number.

Handling disputes

A dated photo set answers the common complaint of never delivered or delivered damaged without an argument.

Questions

  • Do I need a signature too? Where required, yes. A dated photo of the signed paperwork covers it.
  • Does it work without signal? Yes. Everything is stored on the device and no upload is needed.
