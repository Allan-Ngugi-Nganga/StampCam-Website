Free vs paid timestamp camera apps

Updated 5 October 2026 · 4 min read

Free is not really free, and a subscription is not automatically better. What matters is what you give up in each model.

What free usually costs

  • Adverts, including in the capture flow.
  • Watermarks on your own photos, sometimes the app's brand.
  • Feature limits on report export.
  • Data collection to fund it.

What subscriptions usually buy

  • No adverts and more features.
  • A recurring cost, often $60 to $180 a year.
  • Access that ends when you stop paying.

What a one-time purchase gives

  • Every feature, permanently, for a single price.
  • No recurring charge and nothing to cancel.
  • Usually a clearer privacy story, since there is no need to fund servers.

Choosing for documentation

If the app is for work, the cost of a subscription over a couple of years usually exceeds a one-time purchase, and the privacy question is sharper. StampCam is a one-time $19.99 with everything unlocked and nothing uploaded.

Questions

  • Are there genuinely free timestamp cameras? Yes, but they tend to trade adverts, uploads or feature limits for the free price.
  • Why do some apps need a subscription? Often to fund servers. An app that processes on device has less reason to charge monthly.
