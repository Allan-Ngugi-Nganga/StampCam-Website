Timestamped photos for legal documentation

Updated 5 October 2026 · 5 min read

Legal and compliance work rewards consistency. The same rules applied on every occasion produce a record that reads as deliberate, not retrofitted.

Start with a fixed routine

Decide in advance what you capture: an overview, the specific item, and a wider shot for context. Apply the same routine every time so two visits can be compared directly.

Protect the timestamp

  • Keep the phone clock on automatic.
  • Use a network time check so a changed clock is flagged.
  • Never edit or crop after capture.
  • Keep a consistent date format.

Keep the record together

Photos scattered across a camera roll are hard to present. Export a dated PDF per matter, with a cover sheet and the photos in order, and store it with the case file.

Privacy

For sensitive matters, on-device processing matters. If the app uploads photos to a server, the record is out of your hands. StampCam works fully on the phone, so nothing leaves the device.

Questions

  • Are timestamped photos admissible in court? Admissibility depends on the court and the matter. A clear, unedited, dated record supports the case, but it is not a guarantee.
  • Should I keep the original files? Yes. Keep originals alongside any exported report.
