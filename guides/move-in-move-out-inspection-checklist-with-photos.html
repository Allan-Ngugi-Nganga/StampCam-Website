Move-in and move-out inspection checklist with photos

Updated 5 October 2026 · 5 min read

Deposit disputes come down to one question: what did the property look like on the day. A dated, room-by-room photo record answers it.

Shoot in a fixed order

Go room by room in the same order at move-in and move-out, so the two sets line up. Door, walls, floor, fittings, then any appliances.

What to capture in each room

  • An overview from the doorway.
  • Walls and skirting, including any marks.
  • Floors, including any stains or damage.
  • Fittings, fixtures and appliances.
  • Readings: meters, and anything relevant to the tenancy.

Turn it into a document

Export a dated PDF per inspection, with a cover sheet, and keep it with the tenancy file. Two comparable documents settle a dispute in minutes.

Questions

  • How many photos is enough? Four to eight per room is typical: an overview plus the details that matter.
  • Should the tenant get a copy? Yes. Sharing the dated document up front removes most disagreement later.
