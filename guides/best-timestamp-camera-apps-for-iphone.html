Choosing a timestamp camera app for iPhone

Updated 5 October 2026 · 5 min read

There is no single best app for everyone, but there is a small set of criteria that separates a tool you can rely on from a toy with an overlay.

Judge an app on these

  • Data set. Date, time, coordinates, altitude and heading, plus notes.
  • Verified time. A network time check, so a changed clock is visible.
  • Reports. A clean, dated PDF you can hand to a client or insurer.
  • Privacy. Does it upload your photos, or process on device.
  • Price model. One-time purchase versus a rolling subscription.
  • Field use. Battery, offline behaviour and speed at capture.

Where StampCam fits

StampCam is built around those criteria: a full data set burned into the image, a network time check, on-device processing with no account, branded PDF reports, and a one-time price of $19.99 with no subscription.

How to compare fairly

Install two or three apps and take the same photo with each. Compare the stamp, the report and the privacy policy, and check what happens to your data and your wallet after the first year.

Questions

  • Is a free timestamp app good enough? Sometimes for casual use. For work and documentation, the paid models usually trade adverts and uploads for a fee, so choose deliberately.
  • What is the single most important feature? A verifiable timestamp. Everything else is secondary.
