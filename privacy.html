Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

StampCam is built on a simple principle: what happens on your iPhone stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, it collects nothing and sends nothing to us.

The short version: StampCam has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no third-party tracking. Your photos, location and notes are processed and stored only on your device.

Information we collect

None. StampCam does not collect personal information, usage statistics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers or any other data. There is no account to create and no sign-in.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Camera. Used to capture the photos you take. Images are stored on your device.
  • Location. Used only to stamp coordinates, altitude and heading onto your photos. Location data is processed on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any server.
  • Photo library. Used so you can select photos to include in a PDF report. Photos remain on your device.
  • Microphone and speech. Used only for voice-to-text note dictation, handled by iOS on your device.
  • Network. Used only for the optional NTP time check, which contacts a public time server to verify your clock. No personal data is sent during that check.

Where your data is stored

All photos, reports, notes and settings created in StampCam are stored locally on your device. Because there is no backend, we cannot access, view or recover your data.

Third parties

StampCam includes no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. Your data is not shared, sold or disclosed to anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because everything lives on your device, you are in full control. Deleting a photo or report in the app, or deleting the app, removes the associated data. We hold no copy.

Children's privacy

StampCam is a professional tool that is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@stampcam.app.
