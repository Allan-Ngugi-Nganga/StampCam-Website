What makes a claim photo survive review

An adjuster is judging trust as much as damage: the wrong date, a stripped location or an edited image delays a claim

What adjusters want to see

  • A visible date and time, consistent across the set
  • Coordinates and a place reference
  • Images that have not been edited after capture
  • Overview, detail and serial numbers for each item
  • A single report filed with the claim

Why StampCam

StampCam burns verified date, time, GPS coordinates, altitude and heading onto every photo, keeps the record on device, and exports a branded PDF report on the spot. It is a one-time purchase of $19.99 with no subscription and no account.

Questions

  • Is a timestamped phone photo acceptable as evidence? It is much stronger than a plain photo because the time and place are visible in the image. Confirm the requirements of the specific claim.
  • Is any claim data uploaded? No. StampCam has no servers and no account, so claim photos and locations stay on your device.
