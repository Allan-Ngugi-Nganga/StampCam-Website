Photograph progress, defects and deliveries with a verified date, time and location burned into every image, then hand over a branded PDF the same day.

Why site photos get disputed

Contractors label but a wide shot, mid shot, defect close up and a delivery on the wrong date

What a site record should capture

  • Progress from consistent angles so change is obvious
  • Defects and snags with context, not just close ups
  • Safety signage, barriers and PPE
  • Materials on arrival, with condition and serial numbers
  • Existing conditions near neighbouring property

Why StampCam

StampCam burns verified date, time, GPS coordinates, altitude and heading onto every photo, keeps the record on device, and exports a branded PDF report on the spot. It is a one-time purchase of $19.99 with no subscription and no account.

Questions

  • Does StampCam work without signal on site? Yes. Everything is processed on device. A connection only helps with the time check and the GPS fix.
  • Can I keep jobs separate? Yes. Hashtags such as #NorthWing group photos into projects automatically.
