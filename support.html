Support

We read every message and usually reply within two business days.

StampCam is a focused tool, and so is its support. If something is not working, the answers below cover the common cases. If you still need a hand, email us and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Contact: support@stampcam.app

Frequently asked

  • Is it really a one-time purchase? Yes. $19.99 once, every feature unlocked, no recurring fees.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. All processing is on device. A connection only helps with the NTP time check and the GPS fix.
  • The timestamp looks wrong. Turn on the NTP time check so the clock is verified against network time, and confirm your iPhone date is set to automatic.
  • My coordinates are missing. Location permission is required. Open Settings, find StampCam, and allow location access while using the app.
  • Where are my photos and reports? On your device. StampCam has no cloud, so nothing is synced or stored elsewhere.

Troubleshooting

  1. Update to the latest version of StampCam from the App Store.
  2. Restart the app, and if needed restart your iPhone.
  3. Check Settings for StampCam and make sure camera and location permissions are enabled.
  4. Still stuck? Email a short description and, if possible, a screenshot.

Report a bug or request a feature

Email support@stampcam.app with a clear subject line. Bug reports and feature requests both go to the same place and both get read.
