Questions

Good to know

What is a GPS timestamp camera?

A GPS timestamp camera captures photos with the date, time and location permanently embedded in the image. StampCam stamps the date, time, GPS coordinates, altitude and compass heading onto every shot and verifies the clock against network time, so the image itself carries the record of when and where it was taken.

Is StampCam a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase of $19.99 with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no recurring fees. Every feature is unlocked permanently.

Does StampCam work offline?

Yes. It is a fully native app and all photos, reports and data are handled on your device. A connection is only useful for the NTP time check and for GPS fixes, and the app keeps working without either.

Where are my photos and location stored?

Strictly on your iPhone. StampCam has no backend servers, so nothing is uploaded, sold or shared, and there are no analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 17 or later. Live Activities and Lock Screen widgets use iOS 17 capabilities.

Why does the timestamp matter for documentation?

StampCam stamps the photo itself with the date, time, coordinates, altitude and heading, and can verify the clock against network time. That produces a consistent record of when and where a photo was taken, which is what insurers, clients and legal teams ask for.

Can I customise how the stamp looks?

Yes. You control the date format, font size, font colour and which corner the watermark sits in, and you can add multi-line notes to any shot.
